Tunnel Vision

Opacity 40%

Color

Press alt-l to turn the tunnel-vision-screen on and off.
You can also click Tunnel Vision icon and then click the Screen On/Off button.
alt + up arrow to move the highlighted range up.
alt + down arrow to move the highlighted range down.
You can also use alt + j and alt + k.
When the screen is on, you may click on the text to highlight that line.
You may click and drag a selection of text to highlight that selection.
If you select text while the screen is off, that text will be highlighted when the screen is turned on.
